# Returns and refunds

Every print is made to order. Nothing sits in a warehouse: when you place an order, your print is produced just for you and shipped straight from our print partner. Because of that, we can't accept returns or exchanges for change of mind, wrong size chosen, or a print that looks a little different on your wall than it did on your screen. Please check sizes and finishes before you order, and write to us if you're unsure.

## Damaged, defective, or wrong items

If something goes wrong, we make it right. You're covered for a free replacement or a full refund if:

- Your print arrived damaged (bent corners, creases, dents, scratches, torn packaging that reached the print)
- Your print has a production defect (misprint, color banding, wrong finish, frame flaws)
- You received the wrong item, size, or quantity

To claim, write to {soporte} within 30 days of delivery and include:

- Your order number (it starts with LM-)
- Two or three clear photos of the problem, including the packaging if it was damaged in transit
- Whether you'd prefer a replacement or a refund

We'll review the claim, confirm it with our print partner, and either put a replacement into production (usually within 2 business days) or refund your original payment method. Refunds usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank. You don't need to ship the damaged item back.

## Cancellations

Write to {soporte} as soon as possible. We can cancel for a full refund until your order goes into production, which is usually the same day.

## How to contact us

Email {soporte} with your order number. We answer within 1 to 2 business days.

This policy doesn't limit any rights you may have under applicable consumer protection law.

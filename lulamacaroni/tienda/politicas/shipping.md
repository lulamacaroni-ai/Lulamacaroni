# Shipping

## Where we ship

We ship within the United States only, to all 50 states. Deliveries to PO boxes and APO/FPO/DPO addresses depend on the carrier our print partner selects; if your address can't be served, we'll contact you before your order goes into production. We don't offer international shipping yet. If you're outside the US and would love a print, write to {soporte} so we know where to expand next.

## How long it takes

Every print is made to order, so there are two steps before it reaches you:

- Production: 2 to 5 business days
- Shipping: 3 to 8 business days, depending on your location (framed prints can take a little longer)

Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding US holidays. Around major holidays (Thanksgiving through New Year's, in particular) production and carrier times can run longer, so please order early if your print is a gift.

## Tracking

As soon as your order ships, you'll receive an email with a tracking number. Tracking can take 24 to 48 hours to show its first scan.

## Lost or delayed packages

If your tracking hasn't updated in 7 business days, or the package shows "delivered" but you can't find it, please:

- Check with household members, neighbors, and your building's mail room
- Wait 48 hours, since carriers sometimes mark packages delivered a day or two early
- Then write to {soporte} with your order number

If the carrier confirms the package is lost in transit, we'll send a free replacement. Packages marked delivered to the correct address that can't be located are handled case by case.

## Wrong address

Orders ship to the address entered at checkout, so please check it carefully. If you notice a mistake, email us right away: we can change the address only before the order goes into production. If a package comes back because of an incorrect address, we'll contact you to reship it, and a reshipping fee may apply.

## Multiple items

If you order several prints, they may ship separately and arrive on different days. You'll get a tracking number for each package.

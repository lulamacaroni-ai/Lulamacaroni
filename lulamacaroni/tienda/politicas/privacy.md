# Privacy policy

{marca} is the online art shop of artist Lorena Vazquez, operated by {razon_social}, a US limited liability company ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect when you visit or buy from {dominio}, how we use it, and your choices.

## What we collect

- **Order information:** your name, shipping address, email, and the items you bought.
- **Payment information:** payments are processed by Stripe on Stripe's own checkout page. Your card details go directly to Stripe; we never see or store your full card number.
- **Messages:** anything you send us by email.
- **Your cart:** one cookie that remembers what's in your cart. It holds only product codes and quantities.

## How we use it

- To make and ship your order, send its confirmation and tracking, and help you with it
- To handle replacements, refunds, and claims
- To comply with tax, accounting, and legal obligations

We don't send marketing emails unless you've signed up for them.

## Who we share it with

Only the services needed to run the shop:

- **Prodigi**, our print partner, receives your name, shipping address, and order contents to make and ship your print
- **Shipping carriers** chosen by Prodigi receive your name and address for delivery
- **Stripe** processes your payment and calculates sales tax, under its own privacy policy
- **Resend** delivers our order emails
- Accountants, legal advisors, or authorities when the law requires it

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it with anyone for their own marketing.

## Your rights, including California residents

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask us to correct or delete it, and you won't be treated differently for asking. We don't sell or share personal information for advertising. To make a request, email {soporte} with "Privacy request" in the subject. We'll verify your identity and answer within the legal timeframe. Residents of other states with privacy laws can use the same contact.

## Data retention

We keep order records as long as tax and accounting rules require (typically 7 years) and delete or anonymize other data when it's no longer needed.

## Children

Our store is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the latest version is always posted here. Contact: {razon_social}, {domicilio}, {soporte}.
